WHAT FIGMA DOES
- A boolean group (BOOLEAN_OPERATION: UNION / SUBTRACT / INTERSECT / EXCLUDE) has its own Appearance → Corner radius (cornerRadius) and corner smoothing. They act on the RESULT's outline, not on the children.
- The radius rounds EVERY corner of the result outline — convex and concave alike — each clamped to what fits (half of the shorter adjacent edge). Radius 999 = "as round as possible".

  Union r20: the bump on top (B above A) is rounded at its top (15 = half of B's 30-high part) and the joins into A's top edge are concave arcs (radius 15).
  Exclude r20: two sub-paths (A minus B's overlap, B's top part); each rounded; corners on the shared cut line are rounded inside each piece.
  Intersect r999: 60x170 → a pill (radius 30 = half the width).
- Clamp rule matches Figma's rectangle rule: for each vertex r = min(radius, half of each adjacent edge length) — the same per-corner clamp as vector "Corner radius" on a path.
- Corner smoothing (squircle, 0–1) applies to the result the same way (0.6 → the iOS-style curves on every corner).
- Fill: the boolean group's OWN fills draw the result (default #D9D9D9 when made by the Plugin API; Figma's UI keeps the top child's fill on creation). Children's fills don't show.
- Children keep their own radius: a child with radius 40 inside a boolean with radius 0 keeps its 40 corners in the result, and the cut's corners stay sharp.
- Nested booleans: the outer boolean's radius rounds the final outline once (an inner boolean's result is just geometry to it).
- Flatten (⌥⇧F / ⌘E) bakes the rounded result into a VECTOR: the arcs become cubic curves; cornerRadius on the vector is 0.
- Panel: the boolean group's header has the boolean split button (op icon + chevron menu Union / Subtract / Intersect / Exclude / Flatten) and Edit object; Appearance shows Opacity, Corner radius (+ individual-corners button disabled for booleans — one value) and corner smoothing under the radius' settings.
