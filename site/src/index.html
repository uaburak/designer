Design, together, on your desktop.

A design app for macOS and Windows: auto layout, components, variables, prototypes and Dev Mode, with AI on the tools you choose.

Build it once

Components with variants and properties. Change the main one and every instance follows.

One value, every mode

Colours, numbers and strings as variables, with a value for Light and for Dark.

Feedback in place

Pin a comment to the spot, reply in a thread, and resolve it when it’s done.

Working together

One workspace for the whole team.

Invite your team, keep work in shared projects, and open the same file together: everyone’s cursor, every change, live.

Design

Layouts that hold together.

Stack, space and pad with auto layout, and the frame follows its content. Change the gap once and every card moves with it.

Prototype

Make it move.

Connect frames with a drag, choose the trigger and the animation, and press Play. Smart animate matches layers by name and moves them for you.

Dev Mode

From design to code.

Developers get their own view: the distances between layers, code for what they select, and a clear mark on what is ready for development.

AI and Studio

Bring your own AI.

Claude Code, Codex or a local model builds in your file with real layers. Studio hands one request to several agents at once.

Text goes to the agent you pick, pictures to the image route you pick. Wecra has no AI server of its own.
