AI where you design. On your terms.

Actions, image tools, visual search, agents and Studio, running on the providers you choose: the coding agents already on your computer, a local model, or your own keys.

Nothing runs until you set it up

AI actions

Tidy, write and wire up, in one step each.

Press ⌘K for Actions. Rename layers turns “Frame 12” into names that mean something. Shorten, Rewrite and Translate change the selected text, Replace content fills placeholders with real copy, and Add interactions connects your frames.

Every run is a single undo step.

Image tools

Edit the picture where it sits.

Select an image and its toolbar appears. Remove the background, boost the resolution, erase or isolate an area, and vectorize it, all on your own computer. Make an image, edit it with a prompt or expand it through the image route you picked.

Remove background, Boost resolution and Erase download their models once, on first use. Vectorize is Wecra’s own tracer, in the engine.

Visual search

Find it by what it looks like.

Search every frame and component in the files you’ve opened with a few words, a selected layer or a picture. Exact names come first, then what looks or reads the same.

The index is made on your computer while it’s idle, and stays in your workspace.

Agents and MCP

Agents that build in your file.

Chat with Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Cursor or a local model. They read your file and make real layers through Wecra’s MCP tools, and you watch each step land.

Add skills with /, and connectors that ask before they act.

Studio

One request. Several agents at once.

Keep each brand’s products and pictures in a library. Ask for a post and a story: the manager reads the library, asks which image model to use, and hands the frames and pictures to several agents in parallel.

Then it reviews what came back and asks before it fixes anything.

Credits and privacy

Your providers. Your keys. Your balance.

Text goes to the agent you pick, images to the route you pick, and local models stay on your computer. Keys live in your system keychain. A monthly AI balance counts every run, and stops when it runs out if you want it to.

Your prompts, files and pictures never reach Wecra. For files in an organization, only the daily credit totals are reported, so its admins can see the usage.
