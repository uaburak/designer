AI and Studio

AI where you design.

Image tools, agents and Studio, running on the providers you choose: the coding agents already on your computer, a local model, or your own keys.

AI tools

Make and edit images in place.

Describe a picture and it fills the selected layer. Then edit it with a prompt, remove its background or vectorize it, without leaving the canvas.

  • Make an image, Edit with prompt
  • Remove background, Boost resolution, Expand
  • Vectorize in full color, grayscale or black and white
Agents

Agents that work on the canvas.

Chat with Claude Code, Codex or a local model in the Agents panel. The agent reads your file and builds real frames, auto layout and styles, and you watch each step land.

  • Your own CLI agents and local models
  • Skills and connectors you add
  • A monthly AI balance you set
Studio

A manager for your brand's posts.

Keep each brand's pictures and details in a library. Ask for a post and a story: the manager reads the library, hands the work to several agents at once, checks what comes back, and asks before it fixes anything.

  • Brands and products with their pictures
  • Tasks running in parallel, each with its own card
  • A review of the results before you use them

Bring your own AI.

Wecra connects to the tools on your computer. Nothing runs until you set it up.
