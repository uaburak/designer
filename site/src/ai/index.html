Nothing runs until you set it up

AI where you design. On your terms.

Actions, image tools, visual search, agents and Studio, running on the providers you choose: the coding agents already on your computer, a local model, or your own keys.

AI actions

Tidy, write and wire up, in one step each.

Open Actions from the toolbar, or with ⌘K, and pick what to do. Rename layers turns “Frame 12” into names that mean something. Shorten, Rewrite and Translate change the selected text, Replace content fills placeholders with real copy, and Add interactions connects your frames.

Every run is a single undo step.

Image tools

Edit the picture where it sits.

Select an image and its toolbar appears. Remove background takes the background away and keeps the subject, on your own computer, and so do Boost resolution, Erase, Isolate and Vectorize. Make an image, edit it with a prompt or expand it through the image route you picked.

Remove background, Boost resolution and Erase download their models once, on first use. Vectorize is Wecra’s own tracer, in the engine.

Visual search

Find it by what it looks like.

Describe what you’re after, or start from a selected layer or a picture. Visual search looks through the frames and components of the files you’ve opened: components first, then other designs that look or read the same, even when no word matches. Preview one, open its file or insert it.

The index is made on your computer while it’s idle, and stays in your workspace.

Agents and MCP

Agents that build in your file.

Chat with Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Cursor or a local model. They read your file and make real layers through Wecra’s MCP tools, and you watch each step land.

Add skills with /, and connectors that ask before they act.

Studio

One request. Several agents at once.

Keep each brand’s products and pictures in a library. Ask for a post and a story: the manager reads the library and hands the frames and their pictures to several agents at once. Each lands on your canvas as its task is done.

It asks which image model to use the first time, reviews what came back, and asks before it fixes anything.

Credits and privacy

Your providers. Your keys. Your balance.

In Settings › AI, choose who does the work: a coding agent already on your computer, a local model like Ollama, or an image API with your own key, kept in your system keychain. A monthly AI balance counts every run, and stops when it runs out if you want it to.

Your prompts, files and pictures never reach Wecra. For files in an organization, only the daily credit totals are reported, so its admins can see the usage.
